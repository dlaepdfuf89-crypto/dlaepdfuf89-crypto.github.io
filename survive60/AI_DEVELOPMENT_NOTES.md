## How AI was used

The creator directed the game design, progression, visual presentation and release decisions, and used **ChatGPT extensively to produce and revise the game implementation and browser release**. This included the 60-second survival loop, drag-to-move controls, auto-attacks, enemies and bosses, upgrades, combat UI, and integration work. The human creator reviewed the live game and repeatedly requested changes based on actual gameplay.

The gameplay is a real, playable application—not an AI-generated trailer or a mock-up. It is a one-finger, portrait-oriented space survival shooter: move a ship to dodge incoming fire while weapons attack automatically; survive, upgrade, and challenge the boss. The browser edition is a free playable version; the platform editions include platform-specific monetization options.

**Evidence scope:** This is a creator-maintained development note explaining the AI-assisted workflow. It is not a claim that individual commits or a measured percentage of code were independently audited. Production source and credentials are not disclosed here.
