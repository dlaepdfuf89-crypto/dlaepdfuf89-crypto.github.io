Why 60 seconds?

We wanted an action game that makes its promise immediately: survive one intense minute. You do not need an account or a lengthy tutorial to find out what is at stake. Enemy waves increase the pressure, weapon upgrades change how your ship fights, and the Void Reaper arrives to make the final moments count.

Survive 60 is not a passive timer. Your ship attacks automatically while you focus entirely on movement: reading bullet patterns, finding gaps, and deciding where the next safe opening might appear.

How to play

  • On a phone: drag your finger in the game area to steer. Weapons fire automatically.
  • On a computer: use WASD or the arrow keys, or hold the mouse button and drag to move.
  • Survival: make it through the 60-second challenge and battle the boss.
  • Boss Raid, Endless, and Daily Challenge: explore different pacing and ways to improve your personal best.

Bring a friend

The Playgama Sandbox edition includes a friend-challenge sharing button in the lobby and after a run. If you reach the end—or lose a close battle—send the link and see whether someone can beat your result. The game runs in the browser, so there is no app installation required for this edition.
