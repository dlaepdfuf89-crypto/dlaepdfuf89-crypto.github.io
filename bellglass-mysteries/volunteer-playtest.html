Player B

Witness accounts and other sources that can contradict—or validate—Player A's records.

Together

Three acts, a joint worksheet, optional hints, and a solution revealed only after the pair finishes or stops.

We're testing the game, not your detective skills

“Where were you first confused? Did each person contribute? Did the final reveal feel earned—or forced?”

You'll be asked whether you started, finished or stopped, how long preparation and play took, any hints used, and which parts felt unfair, boring or genuinely surprising. There are no required positive ratings or public testimonials. You can withdraw your interest by email.

Questions before applying

Is there a cost?

No. The v0.1.6 developmental playtest invitation is free. Volunteers are not asked to buy, review or subscribe. Applications are not guaranteed to be selected.

Do we need special software or a printer?

The investigator files are PDFs for separate screens or optional print. Having two devices is helpful. A4 or US Letter printing may be used if desired, but you needn't print anything.

How long does it take?

We don't have a reliable human-tested duration yet. Recording how long a real pair spends is an important purpose of this test.

Was the game made with AI?

Yes. AI-assisted writing, visuals and technical preparation have been disclosed. No independent two-adult blind playtest has been confirmed for this expanded version.

Is it the same product sold for $12?

No. The separately published v0.1.5 early-access edition is a paid product. This invitation is for an expanded, unreleased, free developmental v0.1.6 test. Buying anything is unnecessary.
