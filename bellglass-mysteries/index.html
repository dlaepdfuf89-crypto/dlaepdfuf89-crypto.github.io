How two people play

  1. Download the ZIP once and extract it. Choose Screen, A4 or USLetter. Each contains the same case.
  2. Read 01_START_HERE.pdf together. Player A privately reads 02_INVESTIGATOR_A.pdf. Player B privately reads 03_INVESTIGATOR_B.pdf.
  3. Describe clues aloud; compare timestamps. Fill in 04_SHARED_WORKSHEET.pdf together. Both players should contribute evidence.
  4. If stuck, open only 06_OPTIONAL_HINT_01.pdf. Agree on a verdict before opening the separate 99_ANSWER_KEY_OPEN_LAST.pdf.

Bring two screens or print the A/B packets, plus a pen. The entire case, one optional hint, and the complete answer are inside the ZIP. No website is needed once downloaded.
