How two people play

  1. On two devices, use the separate Investigator A and B invitation pages above (no ZIP required). For offline or print play, download the ZIP once, read START_HERE.txt, and select just one edition: Screen, A4 or USLetter.
  2. Read 01_START_HERE.pdf together. Player A privately reads 02_INVESTIGATOR_A.pdf. Player B privately reads 03_INVESTIGATOR_B.pdf.
  3. Describe clues aloud and compare timestamps. Use 04_SHARED_WORKSHEET.pdf as prompts, but record your verdict on paper or in a basic Notes app (this worksheet has no fillable fields). Both investigators should contribute evidence.
  4. If stuck, open only 06_OPTIONAL_HINT_01.pdf. Agree on a verdict before opening the separate 99_ANSWER_KEY_OPEN_LAST.pdf.

Bring two screens or print the A/B packets, plus a pen. The full free case, optional hint, and separate answer are in the ZIP. No website is needed once downloaded. Other event times follow the fair master clock unless a device clock is explicitly named.
